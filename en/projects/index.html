Selected research projects and competitions. I focus on reliability-oriented medical imaging ML, data-centric pipelines, and reproducible evaluation.

FZI Forschungszentrum Informatik, Karlsruhe · Student Research Assistant

07/2025 – Present · OKTI — Retinal OCT Motion-Artifact Correction (3D U-Net + CBAM)
  • Constructed a controllable simulated OCT dataset by augmenting public volumes and injecting synthetic fixational eye-movement trajectories to create paired corrupted/clean training data.
  • Implemented a differentiable warping pipeline with pixel-level lateral displacement labels and volume-level train/val/test splits for supervised learning and fair evaluation.
  • Trained a 3D U-Net + CBAM multi-task model with a reconstruction head (artifact correction) and a shift-estimation head (motion prediction).
  • Designed losses combining masked 3D SSIM + intensity terms and an L1 shift regression loss, with staged weighting to stabilize training.
  • Built a reproducible PyTorch workflow (windowed 3D inputs, mixed precision, early stopping) and added label-audit/QC checks to improve supervision quality.
  • Output: manuscript in preparation (planned submission to IEEE CISA 2026; second author).
07/2024 – 06/2025 · HybridVITA — Teledermatology Segmentation & Scale-Aware Lesion Quantification
  • Curated and preprocessed smartphone dermatology images; implemented illumination-robust preprocessing (e.g., white balance) and data augmentation to improve generalization.
  • Fine-tuned an FCN-ResNet50 (PyTorch) for multi-class segmentation (background / healthy skin / psoriasis) and designed post-processing to obtain clinically usable ROI masks.
  • Integrated segmentation outputs into a telemedical longitudinal monitoring pipeline to enable consistent region extraction for downstream analysis across sessions.
  • Implemented an OpenCV ArUco-based scale calibration module for lesion area quantification, supporting follow-up comparison in remote monitoring.
  • Prepared analyses and communicated model behavior/failure cases to clinical collaborators; contributed technical content to the associated publication.

Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT) · M.Sc. · Information Systems

11/2024 – 04/2025 · Master Thesis — Designing an Iterative Prompt Optimization System for Domain-Specific Chatbots
  • Designed and implemented an iterative multi-agent debating system to refine prompts for a mental-health support chatbot.
  • Grounded the system in Design Science Research, with design principles informed by Cognitive Load Theory and Adaptive Structuration Theory.
  • Automated synthetic dialogue generation and GPTScore-based evaluation, keeping domain experts in the loop via an interactive web interface.
  • Conducted a mixed-methods user study (experts & lay users; PHQ-9, NASA-TLX, qualitative feedback) and derived prescriptive design implications.
11/2023 – 03/2024 · Behavioral Activation Chatbot (RAG + Fine-tuned GPT-3.5)
  • Developed a BA chatbot using a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline and a fine-tuned GPT-3.5 model.
  • Implemented mood tracking, personalized activity recommendations, incentive mechanisms, and schedule management via the Google Calendar API.
  • Ran a SurveyMonkey user study (n = 25) to evaluate usability and self-management impact, showing overall positive effects.
11/2023 – 03/2024 · Engineering Information Systems (EIS) Capstone — Multimodal Workload Analytics
  • Collected and analyzed multimodal data (eye-tracking, HR, PPG, accelerometer, mouse signals) on an ABB operator-training simulator to study workload and performance.
  • Designed analyses and visual reports to provide feedback on stress, attention, and performance.
  • Prototyped a biosignal-adaptive smartwatch interface for integrating physiological signals into everyday work support.
11/2023 – 03/2024 · KG-Mistral — Knowledge-Graph-Augmented QA [1]
  • Implemented entity/relation extraction from questions and querying via SPARQL over a domain KG.
  • Verbalized relevant triples and performed prompt engineering for grounding.
  • Evaluated with SBERT similarity, ROUGE, and BLEU.
  • Improved factuality and grounding of the KG-Mistral pipeline versus baselines.
