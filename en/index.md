I hold an M.Sc. in Information Systems from **Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT)** and have applied research experience at **FZI Forschungszentrum Informatik**.

My work has included biomedical imaging and reconstruction, longitudinal clinical modelling, knowledge-grounded language systems, and expert-in-the-loop AI. Across these projects, I have worked on reproducible research workflows, structured evaluation, and reliability across data and model pipelines.

## Selected Research

### Volumetric OCT Reconstruction & Motion Estimation

Developed a simulation-based 3D workflow for retinal OCT motion-artifact correction and displacement estimation, including differentiable warping, synthetic supervision, quality-control checks, and quantitative evaluation.

[Publication record](/en/publications/)

### Reliable Clinical Trajectory Modelling

Built a public research prototype for temporally valid longitudinal clinical modelling, with patient-level splitting, prediction-time availability checks, executable leakage tests, compact Transformer models, and reproducible reference results.

[Code](https://github.com/Mingze101/reliable-clinical-trajectory-modelling)

### Telemedical Dermatology & Perfusion Analysis

Developed image-segmentation and quantitative-analysis workflows for smartphone dermatology data, including lesion segmentation, longitudinal region extraction, scale-aware measurement, and perfusion analysis.

[Publication](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11166425)

### Evidence-Grounded Clinical Timeline Extraction

Developed a provenance-aware workflow for structuring longitudinal oncology reports into patient timelines while preserving source evidence, temporal information, uncertainty, and consistency checks.

### Expert-in-the-Loop Multi-Agent System

Designed and evaluated a controllable multi-agent system for iterative prompt refinement, combining simulated dialogues, automatic evaluation, expert feedback, manual intervention, and version rollback.

[Master's Thesis](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1pd7S7-Mso8ApdXMqBbO5F84Z1c9DL7mu/view?usp=sharing)

### KGMistral — Knowledge-Graph-Grounded Question Answering

Built an RDF/SPARQL-based retrieval pipeline that maps natural-language questions to structured knowledge-graph evidence and grounds language-model responses in retrieved triples.

[Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=JzL0qm3YA8) · [Code](https://github.com/Mingze101/KGMistral)
